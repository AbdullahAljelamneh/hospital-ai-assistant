# Hospital Policies & Procedures

## Visiting Hours
General visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. ICU patients may receive visitors for 15-minute intervals, twice per day, at 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Pediatric ward allows one parent to stay overnight. All visitors must check in at the reception desk and wear a visitor badge.

## Appointment Scheduling
New patient appointments require a referral from a general practitioner unless visiting the Emergency Department or Walk-in Clinic. Follow-up appointments can be scheduled directly through the hospital's scheduling system. Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a 50 JOD no-show fee. Patients can reschedule up to 3 times per appointment.

## Insurance & Billing
The hospital accepts all major Jordanian insurance providers including MedGulf, Arab Orient, and Jordan Insurance Company. Uninsured patients can apply for the hospital's financial assistance program. Payment plans are available for bills exceeding 200 JOD. Emergency treatment is provided regardless of insurance status per Jordanian law.

## Patient Rights
Patients have the right to access their medical records within 48 hours of request. All patient information is kept confidential under Jordanian healthcare privacy regulations. Patients may request a second opinion at any time. Translation services are available for non-Arabic speakers.

## Medication Policy
Prescriptions are filled at the hospital pharmacy on the ground floor, open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Controlled substances require in-person pickup with valid ID. Refill requests must be submitted 3 business days before the medication runs out. Generic substitutions are made unless the physician specifies brand-name only.

## Emergency Department
The Emergency Department operates 24/7. Patients are triaged using a 5-level system:
- Level 1 (Resuscitation): Immediate life-threatening conditions — seen immediately
- Level 2 (Emergency): Potentially life-threatening — seen within 10 minutes
- Level 3 (Urgent): Serious but not life-threatening — seen within 30 minutes
- Level 4 (Less Urgent): Minor conditions — seen within 1 hour
- Level 5 (Non-Urgent): Minor issues — seen within 2 hours

Chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, and loss of consciousness are always classified as Level 1 or Level 2.
